Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Strategic Workforce Planning Section of the Strategic Talent Management Service, Global Strategy and Policy Division, in the Office of Human Resources of the Department of Management Strategy, Policy and Compliance (DMSPC). The Management Analyst reports to a more senior officer. The focus of this job is the implementation of strategic workforce planning and workforce analytics across the global Secretariat.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Management Analyst will be responsible for the following duties:

•    Contributes to the development, maintenance and updating of standards, procedures, policies, tools and technologies, including those powered by artificial intelligence, for strategic workforce planning and workforce analytics for the United Nations Secretariat.
•    Monitors and analyzes specific aspects of programme/project development and implementation; reviews relevant documents and reports; identifies problems and issues to be addressed and recommends corrective actions; liaises with relevant parties; identifies and tracks follow-up actions.
•    Contributes to developing and implementing innovative solutions, including through the use of artificial intelligence for strategic workforce planning and workforce analytics.
•    Carries out research on current workforce planning and workforce analytics practices, trends and technologies and provides input to strategic workforce planning, which includes collecting, analyzing and presenting quantitative data.
•    Assists in performing assignments, in collaboration with internal stakeholders from headquarters, peacekeeping and humanitarian        entities, to ensure their needs are incorporated in the strategic workforce planning and workforce analysis.
•    Liaises with external stakeholders on strategic workforce planning and workforce analysis matters as directed by the Chief of Section.
•    Prepares various written outputs, e.g. reports, analysis, presentations, and other materials.
•    Provides substantive support to legislative bodies and committees (e.g. ACABQ, Fifth Committee) in workforce planning and workforce      analysis and reporting.
•    Provide guidance to internal stakeholders on substantive policy and procedural issues relating to strategic workforce planning.
•    Participates in the development of training workshops, seminars, etc.; participates in and makes presentations on assigned       topics/activities.
•    Supports the work of the Section in the maintenance and development of knowledge management practices in strategic workforce planning and workforce analysis and reporting, coordinates the dissemination of information among internal and external stakeholders.
•    Performs other duties as required.
 

Competencies
Professionalism: Excellent problem solving capabilities and analytical skills. Ability to identify issues, analyze and participate in the resolution of issues/problems. Ability to assist with data collection using various methods. Ability for quantitative data analysis. Conceptual analytical and evaluative skills to conduct independent research and analysis. Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Excel. Knowledge of the use generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools, including AI agent design and development. Ability to apply judgment in the context of assignments given, plan own work and manage conflicting priorities. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.
 

Communication: Speaks and writes clearly and effectively; listens to others, correctly interprets messages from others and responds appropriately; asks questions to clarify and exhibits interest in having two-way communication; tailors language, tone, style and format to match audience; demonstrates openness in sharing information and keeping people informed.
 

Planning and Organizing: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently.
 
 

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education: A first-level university degree is required; preferably in organizational psychology, data science/analytics, business administration, human resources management, economics, statistics, social science with a quantitative focus, information systems, operations research or a related field.

Experience: Knowledge of MS-Excel is desirable. Drafting and strong analytical skills is desirable. Demonstrated experience in creating AI agents is desirable.

Language: English and French are the two working languages of the United Nations. For this post, fluency in English is required. Knowledge of another UN official language is an advantage.
