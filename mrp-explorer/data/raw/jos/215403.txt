Org. Setting and Reporting
This post is located in the Press and External Relations Section of the UN Information Service in Geneva, Strategic Communications Division of the Department of Global Communications. The Press and External Relations Section is responsible for promoting the work and mission of the United Nations through a range of media-related activities, the development of effective relations with key stakeholders and partners, and outreach activities designed to inform and engage with target audiences, in particular youth. The Associate Public Information Officer reports to the Chief of Press and External Relations.

Responsibilities
Under the supervision of the English Press Editor and the Chief of the Section, the incumbent will be responsible for the following duties:


•Drafts summaries, which are issued as press releases and meeting summaries, on the activities and meetings of human rights intergovernmental bodies and treaty bodies in Geneva, including human rights treaty bodies, the Human Rights Council and the Conference on Disarmament;
•Drafts background releases outlining issues to be reviewed by these bodies prior to their sessions, as well as round-up releases summarizing the main decisions adopted;
•Drafts other types of press releases such as highlights of presentations of UN reports, articles on activities of the United Nations Office at Geneva, and credentials;
•Drafts information material such as summaries of press conferences, briefing notes, leaflets, and other information on the activities of the Organization;

•Supports the Press Editor by editing press releases, meeting summaries and other information material;
•Liaises with the secretariats of UN bodies, in particular within OHCHR, and with the Conference on Disarmament in relation with coverage activities;
•Contributes to outreach activities, including preparing and making presentations to students; assisting with the roll-out of communication campaigns; and promoting international UN Days,
•Drafts content for the website and social media, posts it and monitors social media pages;

•Serves as UNIS focal point on AI matters and represents the Service at DGC AI Lab meetings;

• Provides basic technical support and troubleshooting for web content, digital platforms and multimedia products to ensure their effective functioning and publication. 
•Assists with other public information tasks as required.
