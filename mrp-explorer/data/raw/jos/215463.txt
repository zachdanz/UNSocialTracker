Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Mediation Support Unit (MSU) of the Policy and Mediation Division of the Department of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs (PMD/DPPA).

MSU serves as a system-wide resource that assists the mediation and dialogue initiatives of the UN and partners and provides technical and operational support of mediation and dialogue processes; capacity-building; and mediation guidance, lessons learned and comparative analysis. The Associate Political Affairs Officer will report to the Chief, Mediation Support & Gender, Peace and Security and to the Team Leader of the Mediation Support Unit (MSU).

Responsibilities
Within the delegated authority and under the supervision of the Chief and of the Team Leader of the Mediation Support Unit, the Associate Political Affairs Officer will be responsible for the following:

• Reviews, monitors and assists in providing operational support to on-going or future mediation/dialogue processes in the area of responsibility, ranging from drafting analytical reports on, and coordinating the deployment of, experts in support of mediation processes to assisting colleagues in the development of mediation strategies, assessing mediation support requirements and operational planning needs.

• Assists in supporting mediation-related capacity-building activities in areas of responsibility and mediation-related initiatives with relevant partners.

• Participates in international, regional or national meetings, provides programmatic/substantive expertise on an issue in the area of responsibility, holds programmatic/substantive and organizational discussions with representatives of other institutions.

• Analyses information contained in communications and publications received from different sources, including the press; maintains up-to-date knowledge of events relating to political issues in a specified area or subject matter.

• Assists senior official with briefing notes, background papers and talking points.

• Prepares summaries of articles from a wide variety of sources and draft a variety of correspondence, and sections of reports.

• Monitors political, socio-economic, and other politically relevant developments in an assigned area.

• Provides input into political, humanitarian and economic efforts and programmes.

• Creates databases that track project progress and provides other information relevant to assigned area.

• Performs other related duties as required.

 

Competencies
PROFESSIONALISM: Knowledge and understanding of the work of the United Nations, in particular the Departments of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs and Peace Operations. Shows ability to complete in-depth studies and reach conclusions on possible causes and solutions to political problems in specific areas and/or countries. Shows ability to relate to various issues and perspectives to political, social, economic and humanitarian programmes in affected countries and areas. Shows pride in work and in achievements; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.

TEAMWORK: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others' ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.

PLANNING & ORGANIZING: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education
 

A first-level university degree is required, preferably in political science, international relations, international economics, law, public administration or other related field is desirable.
 

Experience

Experience working for an international organization in the field of mediation, conflict resolution, or political affairs is desirable.

Language:

English and French are the two working languages of the United Nations. For this post, fluency in English is required. Knowledge of another UN official language is an advantage.
