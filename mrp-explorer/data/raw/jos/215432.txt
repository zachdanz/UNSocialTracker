Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Travel Unit, Travel and Transportation Section in the Facilities and Commercial Activities Service, Division of Administration, Department of Operational Support, New York. The incumbent reports directly to the Chief of the Travel Unit.


The Department of Operational Support (DOS) provides operational support to UN Secretariat entities globally, including advisory, operational and transactional support services and, where needed, exercises delegated authority on behalf of clients. DOS supports the entire UN Secretariat, consisting of almost 100 entities located around the globe.


The Facilities and Commercial Activities Service (FCAS) operates the Headquarters complex, striving to provide effective and efficient planning, management, maintenance and operation for all existing physical facilities and assets at the UN Headquarters, New York. FCAS also supports office and conference facilities, property management, travel and transportation services, archives and records management, mail and pouch operations, catering, giftshop and the United Nations Postal Administration.


The Travel and Transportation Section provides travel and transportation services at UN Headquarters (travel, shipments management, UN travel documents (e UNLP & UN Certificates), visas and host country services, local transportation, materials management and UNHQ garage operations.
 

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Travel Officer will be responsible for the following duties:

(a) Travel Business and Systems Support

      i. Travel Business Support

Supports management in monitoring operational performance, including through the use of business intelligence reporting tools;
Liaises with process experts across business and functional areas to define, harmonize and align cross-functional business processes.
      ii. Systems Support

Defines business requirements and establishes priorities for system enhancements;
Ensures that system enhancements are developed in accordance with approved specifications;
Coordinates the testing of system enhancements across the global travel community.
(b) Training

Delivers training to Local Process Experts (LPEs) and end users;
Supports and provides advice on the development and revision of training materials;
Develops user aids and guidance documentation for business processes not covered in detail by Umoja training materials.
(c) Production Support

Provides timely responses to functional inquiries from end users and Local Process Experts (LPEs) across the Secretariat;
Provides intensive support to Local Process Experts, as required;
Contributes to the Community of Practice by responding to frequently asked questions and sharing knowledge and best practices.
(d) Continued Implementation of Umoja Travel in the Secretariat

Conducts change impact assessments by comparing current ("as-is") and future ("to-be") processes and analyzing the implications of change, including changes to roles and responsibilities;
Leads data conversion activities, including data collection, cleansing, enrichment and validation, for all required data types (e.g., master and transactional data) across implementation phases, including mock conversions, dress rehearsals and cutover activities;
Leads testing activities, including Product Integration Testing (PIT) and User Verification Testing (UVT), to validate Umoja travel solutions;
Conducts user access mapping and workflow design, taking into account delegation of authority, departmental responsibilities, user roles and functional skill requirements.
