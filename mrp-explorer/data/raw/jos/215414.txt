Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the United Nations Forum on Forests Secretariat(UNFFS) . The Associate Programme Officer reports to
the Chief of the Policy Development and Implementation Section. The focus of this job is to support the work of the Global Forest Financing Facilitation Network, and data collection, data analyses, digitization and IT work of the UNFF Secretariat related to forest financing. . More
information on UNFFS can be found at http://www.un.org/esa/forests/.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Programme Officer will be responsible for the following duties:
 

Works with key clients to facilitate the development, implementation and evaluation of assigned programmes/projects ; monitors and analyzes programme/project development and implementation on sustainable forest management and the GFFFN; reviews relevant documents and reports and assists in identifying problems and issues to be addressed and recommends corrective actions; liaises with relevant stakeholder; identifies and tracks follow-up actions.
Carries out basic research on selected aspects of programmes, operations related to forest financing and other activities, etc., to include collecting, analyzing and presenting statistical data and other information gathered from diverse sources. 
Provides substantive support for policy coordination and evaluation functions, including the review and analysis of emerging issues and trends participation in evaluations or research activities and studies on all issues related to forests and the work of the UNFF Secretariat. 
Assists in performing consulting assignments, in collaboration with the client, by planning facilitating workshops, through other interactive sessions, and assisting in developing the action plan the client will use to engage more effectively in the work of the UNFF.
 Participates in survey initiatives; assists with design of data collection tools; , reviews,analyzes and interprets responses, regarding financing forests, and the work of the clearing house of the GFFFN, including its website maintenance, update, and development of
additional databases, communicating with countries on these data, and, identifying problems/issues and prepares preliminary conclusions.
Contributes to the preparation of various written outputs of the UNFF Secretariat, e.g. draft background papers, analytical notes, sections of reports and studies, inputs to publications,etc.
 Provides administrative and substantive support to consultative and other meetings, conferences, etc. organized by the Secretariat, to include proposing agenda topics, identifying and proposed participants, preparation of background documents and presentations, handling
logistics, etc. 
Undertakes outreach activities; participates in the development of training workshops, seminars, etc.; participates in and makes presentations on assigned topics/activities.
Participates in field missions, including provision of substantive and administrative support,
data collection, etc.
Coordinates activities related to budget funding (programme/project preparation and submissions, progress reports, financial statements, etc.) and preparation of related documents/reports (pledging, work programme, programme budget, etc.). 
Performs other duties as required.
Competencies
Professionalism  Knowledge and understanding of theories, concepts and approaches relevant to social, economic and environment aspects of sustainable forest management, and forest financing, with strong IT, data collection and analysis, and website management skills, as well as ability to establish effective networking with and among relevant stakeholders. Ability to identify issues, analyze and participate in the resolution of issues/problems. Ability to assist with data collection using various methods. Conceptual analytical and evaluative skills to conduct independent research and analysis, including familiarity with and experience in the use of various research sources, including electronic sources on the internet, intranet and other databases. Ability to apply judgment in the context of assignments given, plan own work and manage conflicting priorities. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations.Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.                                  
Teamwork: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others’ ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.                                                                                                                                           
Planning and organizing: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently.


 
Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education : A first-level university degree is required, preferably in statistics, natural resources management, ecology, economics, or a related field.                            
Experience: A minimum of two years of progressively responsible experience in socioeconomic data collection and analyses is desirable. . Experience in developing of websites, maintaining databases, data collection, data management, information systems, and IT related areas is desirable. Experience in establishing partnership and networking with countries and civil society representatives is desirable.                                                                                                                                                        
Language: English and French are the two working languages of the United Nations. For this post, fluency in English is required. Knowledge of another UN official language is an advantage.
