Org. Setting and Reporting
This post is located in the Asia and the Pacific Division in the Departments of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs and Peace Operations (DPPA-DPO). Under the general supervision of the Director of Asia and Pacific Division, the incumbent reports to a Senior Political Affairs Officer/Team Leader.

Responsibilities
Under the supervision of the Team Leader for South and Southeast Asia, the Associate Political Affairs Officer will be responsible for the following duties:


• Analyses information contained in communications and publications from different sources, including the press; maintains up-to-date knowledge of
developments relating to political issues in a number of countries as assigned, or specified aspects of a subject matter;
• Assists senior officials with briefing notes, background papers and talking points.
• Prepares summaries from a wide variety of sources and drafts a variety of correspondence, and sections of reports.
• Monitors political, socio-economic, and other politically relevant developments in an assigned area.
• Collaborates closely with other team members and provides input into political, humanitarian and economic efforts and programmes.
• Creates databases that track project progress and provides other information relevant to assigned area.
• Helps formulate project proposals and ensures their implementation.
• Performs other related duties as required.

Competencies
Professionalism: Ability to identify and analyze political, ethnic, racial, social and economic problems that cause civil unrest in a country or geographic area. Ability to develop sources for data collection. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.
 

Communication: Speaks and writes clearly and effectively. Listens to others, correctly interprets messages from others and responds appropriately. Asks questions to clarify, and exhibits interest in having two-way communication. Tailors language, tone, style and format to match the audience. Demonstrates openness in sharing information and keeping people informed.
 

Planning and organizing: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies. Identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required. Allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work. Foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning. Monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary. Uses time efficiently.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education:
A first-level university degree in political science, international relations, international economics, law, public administration or other related area is required.
 

Experience:
 

MRP is mandatory for YPP staff with two years of service in their initial assignment. At least one year of YPP service is required.

Experience working on peace and security, or related issues is desirable.
Experience in monitoring political, peace and security and related developments in a geographic area of responsibility is desirable.
Experience in writing documentation for senior officials, correspondence, country specific/regional analysis is desirable.
Experience in organizing large multi-stakeholder meetings and/or events is desirable.
Experience in backstopping field presences is desirable.
Experience in programmatic and administrative work is desirable.

Language: English and French are the two working languages of the United Nations. For
this post, fluency in English is required. Knowledge of another UN official language is an
advantage.
