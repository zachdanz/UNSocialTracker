Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Demographic and Social Statistics Branch of the Statistics Division, Department of Economic and Social Affairs (DESA). The Associate Statistician reports to the Chief of the Social and Gender Statistics Section.

DESA is a vital interface between global policies in the economic, social and environmental spheres and national action. Its work is guided by the universal, integrated and transformative 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, along with a set of 17 Sustainable Development Goals and 169 associated targets adopted by the UN General Assembly on 25 September 2015. 

 

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Statistician will be responsible for the following duties:

Participates in the collection, evaluation, analysis, compilation and dissemination of  social and gender statistics and data from Member States by selecting methods of data collection, selecting and implementing appropriate methods for validating collected data and processing data for incorporation into statistical database(s); 
Assist in capacity development and technical support to countries on social and gender statistics production, and review relevant country practices;
Assist in the development or revision of standards for data and metadata on gender, international migration, time-use and other social statistics, including statistical concepts, definitions and classifications;
Conduct methodological research, analyze, and present information gathered from various data sources on assigned topics; 
Prepare draft technical documents for international, intergovernmental and expert group meetings, and assist in drafting relevant technical reports;
Assist in the organization and conduction of seminars, workshops and expert meetings;
Collaborate with general service and professional staff in the section, branch and division;
Maintain close liaison and cooperation with relevant counterparts in national and international institutions, including headquarters, regional commissions, specialized agencies and national governments;
Perform other duties as assigned.
