Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Contractual Translation Management Unit, Languages Service, Division of Conference Management, Department for General Assembly and Conference Management.  The incumbent will report to the Chief, Contractual Translation Management Unit. 

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Programme Officer will be responsible for the following duties: 
•    Undertakes administrative actions related to contractual translation services and the recruitment of temporary meeting assistants, supporting the effective delivery of the Language Service’s work.: liaises with the Document Management Section and the relevant Language Sections; reviews and approves contracts for contractual contractors in the gDoc contractual module; reviews shopping carts and creates purchase orders in Umoja for individual contractors; monitors and reports on the use of resources earmarked for contractual translation and temporary assistance for meetings.
•    Reviews actions related to administration of human resources activities, e.g., recruitment, placement, promotion, job classification, separation of staff, training, etc., ensuring consistency in the application of UN rules and procedures.
•    Assists Assists Hiring Managers with all job-opening-related matters in Inspira by ensuring the timely circulation of job announcements and the efficient processing of recruitment cases.
•    Reviews and submits cases for classification and job descriptions by liaising with managers on submission of the classification requests.
•    Assists in the development, implementation and evaluation of projects related to contractual translation and temporary assistance for meetings, etc.
•    Monitors and analyzes specific aspects of programme/project development and implementation; reviews relevant documents and reports; identifies problems and issues to be addressed and recommends corrective actions; liaises with relevant parties; identifies and tracks follow-up actions.
•    Carries out basic research on selected aspects of programmes, operations and other activities, etc., to include collecting, analyzing and presenting statistical data and other information gathered from diverse sources.
•    Provides substantive support for policy coordination and evaluation functions, including the review and analysis of emerging issues and trends, participation in evaluations or research activities and studies. 
•    Participates in survey initiatives; assists with design of data collection tools; issues data collection tools, reviews, analyzes and interprets responses, identifies problems/issues and prepares preliminary conclusions.   
•    Contributes to the preparation of various written outputs, e.g. draft background papers, analytical notes, sections of reports and studies, inputs to publications, etc.
•     Prepares financial projections and spending plans for non-post resources allocated to contractual translation and temporary assistance for meetings. 
•    Contributes to the collection and analysis of data as well as identification of trends or patterns and provide draft insights through graphs, charts, tables and reports using data visualization methods for data-driven planning, decision-making, presentation and reporting.
•    Assists the Chief of Unit in planning and managing resources of the Unit as well as supervising the work of General Service staff.
•    Monitors and evaluates performance of staff under supervision.
•    Performs other duties as required.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education: 
A first-level university degree is required; preferably in business administration, management, documents management, contract management or a related field is desirable.

Experience: 
MRP is mandatory for YPP staff with two years of service in their initial assignment. At least one year of YPP service is required.
A minimum of two years of progressively responsible experience in project/programme management, administration, documentation processing or related area is desirable.
Experience with implementation of the CEB-AITC (Chief Executives Board for Coordination and the Association internationale des traducteurs de conférence) agreement, the recruitment of individual contractors (Language professionals) and/or the United Nations rules and regulations governing submission and processing of United Nations documentations is desirable.
Experience in working with ERP (Entreprise Resources Planning) systems used by the United Nations Common System is desirable.

Language: 
English and French are the working languages of the United Nations Secretariat. For this post, English and French are required.
