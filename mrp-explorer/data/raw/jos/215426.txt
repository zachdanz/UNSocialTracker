Org. Setting and Reporting
The Associate Economic Affairs Officer is responsible for conducting economic analysis and research, gathering data, promoting the organization’s positions, and supporting relationships with external stakeholders. The role engages with a wide range of partners throughout the research and policy process—collecting data, consulting on methodologies, disseminating findings, representing the organization, and strengthening collaborative relationships. This post is located in the Demographic Dynamics and Innovation-driven Development Section (DDIDS) in the Sub Regional Office – Western Africa and reports to the Chief of Section under the overall supervision by the Director of the Subregional Office. 

Responsibilities
Support the collection, integration, and analysis of data, contribute to identifying trends or patterns, and prepare draft insights through graphs, charts, tables, and reports using data visualization methods to support data‑driven planning, decision‑making, presentation, and reporting.
Analyze data on regional, sub-regional and national economic indicators, including macroeconomic indicators, using statistical tools in collaboration with internal and external research partners, to generate insights that inform policies and programme design.
Maintain and regularly update socio-economic databases by validating, organizing, and integrating new data to support timely analysis and inform the work of multiple organizational units.
Contribute to econometric and statistical analysis by supporting the interpretation of outputs and providing inputs to scenario planning, evidence-based decision-making, and programme design.
Contribute to cross-sectoral economic analysis and macroeconomic modeling by gathering data from statistics offices, and International Financial Institutions (IFIs), and other relevant data sources, conducting preliminary analysis and providing inputs to support economic planning.
Contribute to the monitoring and analysis of fiscal policy development by analyzing data on agreed indicators using appropriate quantitative methodologies and producing findings.
Contribute to the analysis of the structure and the cost of public debt based on different sources and provide initial findings for policy recommendations.
Contribute to the assessment of international trade and investment flows by analyzing cross-border data and trends and producing initial findings to support insights for regional and global economic cooperation.
Contribute to studies on regional integration, trade corridors, regional value chains, regional investment clusters, and cross-border linkages by analysis and synthesizing findings to support regional development initiatives and cooperation frameworks.
Contribute to the monitoring and assessment of the impact of tariff and non-tariff measures on trade flows and regional integration and provide findings.
Provide data analysis and advisory inputs on labour mobility and migration policies within and between countries by synthesizing evidence to promote inclusion and support regional cooperation.
Contribute to the analysis of industrial and innovation clusters by integrating data, literature, and stakeholder inputs to support regional growth through industry cooperation and new technologies, while also ensuring environmental protection.
Contribute to the development and implementation of models and frameworks that support economic planning and transformation in the context of climate resilience, nature-positive outcomes, pollution reduction, and sustainable natural resource management through conducting data analysis and generating analytical findings.
Contribute to the implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of programmes and projects on economic affairs by supporting coordination, tracking progress, and providing inputs to facilitate timely delivery.
Contribute to policy advisory missions by conducting initial analysis, preparing background materials, and supporting inclusive economic and development planning processes.
Support the integration of regional and sub-regional economic dimensions into national planning processes, institutional frameworks, and policy coordination mechanisms through research and technical assistance.
Contribute to the organization of technical meetings and stakeholder workshops by supporting agenda development, stakeholder coordination, and documentation of discussions to facilitate consensus-building and capture lessons learned.
Competencies
Professionalism: Knowledge of economic theories and their application. Ability to identify and develop sources for data collection. Ability to undertake research, analyse data, make recommendations and write reports. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work. Able to analyze and interpret data in support of decision-making and convey resulting information to management.

Communication:  Speaks and writes clearly and effectively; listens to others, correctly interprets messages from others and responds appropriately; asks questions to clarify, and exhibits interest in having two-way communication; tailors language, tone, style and format to match audience; demonstrates openness in sharing information and keeping people informed.

Teamwork:  Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others’ ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.
