Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights, Regional Office for the Middle East and North Africa (ROMENA), in Beirut, Lebanon. The Human Rights Officer will work under the overall supervision of the Regional Representative and the direct supervision and guidance of a Human Rights Officer at ROMENA. 

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Human Rights Officer will be responsible for the following duties: 

 

Monitor the implementation of international human rights law and international humanitarian law, and support follow-up on recommendations from human rights mechanisms, including treaty bodies, the Universal Periodic Review, special procedures and investigative mechanisms, to advance compliance and accountability.
Collect, document and analyse evidence-based information on civil, cultural, economic, social and political rights, including the right to development, within assigned thematic or geographic areas to support in-depth analysis, early warning, accountability, and informed decision-making, ensuring that human rights data is discoverable, interoperable and appropriately shared, including from gender and leave-no-one-behind perspectives.
Conduct human rights monitoring and field verification missions, including assessments and early-warning exercises, in line with UN standards and methodologies, and draft substantive analytical reports containing findings, trends and actionable recommendations.
Support fact-finding and investigative missions documenting violations of international human rights and humanitarian law, ensuring adherence to victim- and witness-protection standards and facilitating collaboration across components.
Draft, review and contribute to human rights reports, briefings, legal and policy analyses and other communications, and prepare analytical papers that inform strategies, policies, programmes and decision-making, with integration of gender and leave-no-one-behind considerations.
Collect, verify and analyse multi-source information on thematic and country-specific human rights situations, ensuring data quality, methodological compliance and gender-responsive analysis.
Prepare analytical products, including lessons learned and trend analyses, to inform targeted human rights strategies, crisis prevention and response, and policy and programme development.
Provide technical research and substantive support to advisory processes with national counterparts, including reviewing draft legislation, policies and security-sector reforms to ensure alignment with international human rights standards.
Provide substantive professional support to national and regional counterparts in strengthening human rights systems, institutions and frameworks, including complaints mechanisms, victim and witness support services, engagement with international human rights mechanisms, and implementation of recommendations.
Support the integration of international human rights norms and standards into political, peace and security, humanitarian, development and economic frameworks and processes, contributing to efforts to address impunity and structural human rights challenges.
Contribute to advocacy and participatory processes related to intergovernmental negotiations, public forums and peace processes, supporting the integration of human rights and anti-discrimination standards.
Provide technical support on administration of justice and security-sector issues, including trial and detention monitoring, transitional justice initiatives and vetting processes, through analysis and technical inputs.
Contribute to guidance and professional support to civil society actors on integrating human rights into protection, rule-of-law, development, transitional justice and accountability initiatives.
Provide research, coordination and follow-up support to treaty bodies, special procedures, the Universal Periodic Review and commissions of inquiry.
Provide professional research, analysis and policy advisory support on human rights issues related to economic policy and sustainable development, including poverty, inequality and development financing, contributing inputs to UN analytical and cooperation frameworks.
Engage with youth networks to support advocacy for youth rights and meaningful participation in civic, political and decision-making processes.
Support effective communication and public information efforts by contributing drafting, technical and coordination inputs to amplify human rights messaging.
Competencies
PROFESSIONALISM: Knowledge of human rights issues and ability to identify related problems in their political, ethnic, racial, gender equality and socio-economic dimensions. Knowledge of institutional mandates of the Human Rights Council and its subsidiary mechanisms, prevailing policies and procedures. Research and analytical skills, including ability to identify and participate in the resolution of human rights issues. Ability to identify sources for data collection, analyze information and draft human rights reports. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.

 

PLANNING AND ORGANIZING: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently.

 

TEAMWORK: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others' ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education

A first-level university degree is required, preferably in human rights, law, political science, international relations, social sciences, economics, public policy, sustainable development, humanitarian affairs or related field.  


 

Work Experience 

A minimum of 2 years of experience in human rights or related areas is desirable. Relevant experience may include, but is not limited to political affairs, public affairs, international relations, law, social sciences, economics, development, humanitarian affairs, or other related areas. 

Working collaboratively is desirable.

Knowledge of human rights monitoring, documentation, analysis, research and reporting methodologies, including planning, coordinating and conducting field missions is desirable.

Skills in drafting human rights reports, policy and other documents is desirable.

Knowledge of regional and international human rights mechanisms is desirable.

Skills in conducting gender-, age- and trauma-sensitive human rights interviews is desirable.

Skills in designing, delivering, and evaluating capacity-building and training programmes is desirable.

Knowledge of international human rights law, norms, and standards is desirable.

Skill in applying and maintaining confidentiality, integrity, and availability principles is desirable.

English and French are the working languages of the United Nations. For this post, fluency of English is required.
