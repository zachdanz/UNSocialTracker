Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the in the Office of the Director of the Economic Cooperation and Trade Division (ECTD) of the Economic Commission for Europe (ECE), based in Geneva Switzerland. The P2 will report directly to the D1, Chief of Service, Economic Cooperation and Trade Division. This position supports the overall coordination of the Division’s trade-related work stream (ECE Sub-Programme 6) across ECE’s four pillars of activities: normative function, convening platform, analysis and technical assistance. Sub-Programme 6 supports ECE member States to harness the power of trade for sustainable development across the Division’s well-established workstreams. The workstreams encompass: regulatory and procedural barriers to trade, agricultural quality standards, regulatory cooperation and harmonization, trade facilitation, supply chain traceability. In line with ECE’s current cross-cutting priority themes, particular attention is given to the interaction between trade and the circular economy (SDG 12) and the digital and green transformations. Particular attention is also given to ECE’s 17 programme countries with economies in transition (in Central Asia, Southern Caucasus, Western Balkan and Eastern Europe), to empower them to meet challenges and harness opportunities from sustainable and circular trade.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the duties of the Associate Economic Affairs Officer are:

Economic and/or sector analysis:
• Collects, evaluates, interprets and analyses specified statistics and qualitative socio-economic information obtained from a variety of sources and pertaining to trade and sustainable development in the Pan-European region, including ECE’s 17 programme countries with economies in transition (in Central Asia, Eastern Europe, Southern Caucasus and Western Balkan).
• Prepares briefing notes, inputs to speeches and similar material on trade and sustainable development-related issues in a form that is meaningful to non-specialists; 
• Assists in the organization and servicing of expert group meetings, seminars, etc. on trade and sustainable development-related issues.

Intergovernmental support:
• Prepares inputs for reports to intergovernmental bodies.
• Follows intergovernmental meetings and prepares summary reports and similar material.
• Prepares inputs to statements by members of the Bureau and Secretariat staff to such meetings.
• Assists in the organization of panels, round tables etc. on trade and sustainable development-related  issues for inter-governmental processes. 

Technical cooperation:
• Prepares documentation for technical cooperation programmes and projects.
• Backstops the implementation of technical cooperation programmes and projects on trade and sustainable development in ECE’s 17 programme countries with economies in transition (in Central Asia, Eastern Europe, Southern Caucasus and Western Balkan).

General: 
• Performs other related duties as required tasks for the final delivery of the ECE Sub-programme 6 (Trade) work program ;
• Undertakes on-the-job and other training activities, both internally and externally.
