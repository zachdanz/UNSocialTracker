Org. Setting and Reporting
The Independent Institution on Missing Persons in the Syrian Arab Republic (IIMP) was established by the General Assembly in its resolution 77/301, adopted on 29 June 2023. Please note that while this position is currently located in Geneva, Switzerland, the duty station may be subject to change. Applicants should be aware that the position may be relocated to Damascus, Syria, or to another location relevant to the Institution's operations. By applying, candidates confirm their willingness to accept such a reassignment in accordance with UN regulations and rules, should such a decision be made. The incumbent reports to the Human Rights Officer or his/her designee.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Information Analyst may be responsible for the following duties:


Assists with research and the collection of multi-source information concerning missing persons in the Syrian Arab Republic, including through targeted open-source research in Arabic and English, in accordance with established research methodologies and priorities.


Reviews and assesses information and data from multiple sources to determine relevance and reliability, identify gaps and inconsistencies, and support the identification of patterns, links, trends and associations relevant to missing persons cases.


Assists with the compilation, cross-referencing and structuring of information across cases, individuals, incidents, locations and sources, including reviewing, assessing and documenting potential matches across different sources and datasets, and supports the maintenance and updating of relevant databases and information systems in accordance with established procedures.

Contributes to the preparation of analytical summaries, assessments, visualizations, briefings and other information products by compiling and synthesizing relevant findings for review by more senior colleagues.


Participates in meetings and consultations with IIMP colleagues and relevant external partners, as appropriate, to support the collection, exchange and analysis of information concerning missing persons, and keeps abreast of developments relevant to the Syrian context.


Performs other duties as required.

Competencies

Is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results. Is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns. Shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.
 

Planning and organizing: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies. Identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required. Allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work. Foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning. Monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary. Uses time efficiently.
 

Technological awareness: Keeps abreast of available technology. Understands applicability and limitations of technology to the work of the office. Actively seeks to apply technology to appropriate tasks. Shows willingness to learn new technology.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education: A first-level university degree is required; preferably in criminology, law, Middle Eastern studies, conflict or security studies, international relations, human rights or criminal law, political/social science, information management, data or information science, or other related analytical fields.

Experience: Experience in research, information analysis, data analysis, information management, open-source research or a related field is desirable. 
Experience in collecting, reviewing, assessing, organizing and cross-referencing information from multiple sources is desirable.
Experience using databases, analytical software or other information-management tools is desirable.

Language: English and French are the two working languages of the United Nations. For this post, fluency in English and Arabic is required.
