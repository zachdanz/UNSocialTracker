Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Technology and Innovation Policy Research Section of the Technology, Innovation and Knowledge Development Branch in the Division on Technology and Logistics within the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD). The Associate Economic Affairs Officer reports to the Chief of the Technology and Innovation Policy Research Section. 

The Technology and Innovation Policy Research Section is primarily responsible for the co-production and dissemination of the biennial UNCTAD flagship Digital Economy and Innovation Report, production of analytical reports in support of the Commission on Science and Technology for Development (CSTD) and of the biennial science, technology and innovation (STI) issue in the General Assembly, and providing substantive inputs for the senior management of UNCTAD on issues covered by the Section.


Responsibilities
Under the supervision of the Chief of the Technology and Innovation Policy Research Section, the duties of the Associate Economics Affairs Officer will be as follows:

Economic and/or sector analysis: 
•    Contributes to original research and analysis on technology and innovation policy issues, including the Technology and Innovation Report.
•    Collects, evaluates, interprets, analyses specified statistics and qualitative socio-economic information obtained from a variety of sources and pertaining to particular sectors, countries, regions or development topics, as inputs to the Digital Economy and Innovation Report and other analytical outputs of the section. 
•    Prepares briefing notes, inputs to speeches, draft press releases, articles and inputs for social media and similar material science, technology and innovation in a form that is meaningful to non-specialists;  
•    Prepares first drafts of analytical studies and background research for other substantive papers on technology and innovation. 
•    Assists in the organization and servicing of expert group meetings, seminars, etc issues related to science, technology and innovation, including coordination with consultants and resource persons.

Intergovernmental support: 
•    Prepares inputs for reports to intergovernmental bodies.
•    Follows intergovernmental meetings and prepares summary reports and similar material.
•    Prepares inputs to statements by members of the Bureau and Secretariat staff to such meetings.
•    Assists in the organization of panels, round tables etc on development issues for inter-governmental processes.  

Technical cooperation: 
•    Compiles and analyses the data and other information necessary for the preparation of technical cooperation project proposals in the area of economic development.
•    Prepares documentation for technical cooperation programmes and projects.
•    Contributes to the preparation of material for the approval of regional, country or sector technical cooperation projects. 
•    Monitors, backstops and assesses the implementation of technical cooperation programmes and projects.  

General:  
•    Performs other related duties as required, including a variety of administrative tasks necessary for the final delivery of the work unit’s services; 
•    Undertakes on-the-job and other training activities, both internally and externally. 
 

Competencies
•    Professionalism: Knowledge of economic theories and their application. Knowledge of applied empirical research to advise Governments and private sectors. Ability to identify and develop sources for data collection. Ability to undertake research, analyse data, make recommendations and write reports. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.

•    Communication: Speaks and writes clearly and effectively; listens to others, correctly interprets messages from others and responds appropriately; asks questions to clarify, and exhibits interest in having two-way communication; tailors language, tone, style and format to match audience; demonstrates openness in sharing information and keeping people informed.

•    Teamwork: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others’ ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education: A first-level university degree in economics or a related field is required. An advanced university degree (Master’s degree or equivalent) in economics or a related field is desirable.

Experience:

Experience in economic research and analysis, policy formulation, application of economic principles in development programmes or related area is desirable. 
Experience preparing original research and reports related to Technology and Innovation Policy analysis is desirable. 
Experience contributing to United Nations publications is desirable. 
 
Language: English and French are the two working languages of the United Nations. For this post, fluency in English is required. Knowledge of another UN official language is an advantage.
