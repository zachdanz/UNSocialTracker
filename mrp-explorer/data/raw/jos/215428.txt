Org. Setting and Reporting
The Economic and Social Commission for Western Asia (ESCWA) forms part of the United Nations Secretariat and, like the other regional commissions, operates under the supervision of the United Nations Economic and Social Council. ESCWA comprises 21 Arab countries in Western Asia and North Africa. ESCWA provides a framework for the formulation and harmonisation of sectoral policies for member countries, a platform for congress and coordination, a home for expertise and knowledge, and an information observatory. ESCWA activities are coordinated with the divisions and main offices of the Headquarters of the United Nations, specialised agencies, and international and regional organizations, including the League of Arab States and its subsidiary bodies, and the Gulf Cooperation Council. 

This position is in the External Relations and Communications Section (ERC). ERC is headed by the Secretary of the Commission and handles the Commission’s relationships with Member States and development partners, and communication with the media and the public. It covers the following three main areas of work: external relations, technical cooperation, and media and outreach. The Associate Programme Management Officer reports to the Secretary of the Commission.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Programme Management Officer will be responsible for the following duties: 


•    Supports the Secretary of the Commission in coordinating ESCWA’s engagement in regional and country-level United Nations processes; liaises with substantive divisions and relevant United Nations counterparts to consolidate institutional inputs, identify opportunities for collaboration and monitor follow-up on agreed actions.
•    Provides substantive and organizational support to ESCWA’s role in the Joint Secretariat of the Arab States Regional Collaborative Platform and evolving regional coordination arrangements; contributes to the preparation and monitoring of joint workplans, consolidation of inputs from participating entities and Issue-Based Coalitions, and preparation of system-wide results reports.
•    Facilitates ESCWA’s engagement with Resident Coordinators, Resident Coordinator Offices and United Nations Country Teams; reviews country priorities and requests for support, liaises with relevant ESCWA divisions to facilitate access to regional expertise, and tracks the delivery and follow-up of country-level support.
•    Reviews and consolidates ESCWA’s contributions to Common Country Analyses, United Nations Sustainable Development Cooperation Frameworks and related planning, monitoring and reporting processes; identifies opportunities to integrate regional perspectives, normative frameworks and ESCWA’s analytical and technical expertise.
•    Carries out research and analysis on emerging development trends, regional priorities and United Nations reform initiatives; collects and synthesizes information from diverse sources to support policy coordination, institutional positioning and senior management decision-making.
•    Contributes to the preparation and review of briefing notes, talking points, background papers, presentations, analytical reports and official correspondence for senior management; reviews inputs for accuracy, coherence and consistency with institutional priorities and relevant mandates.
•    Provides substantive and administrative support to high-level regional meetings, inter-agency consultations and intergovernmental processes, including the preparation of agendas, meeting documentation and presentations; coordinates inputs and logistical arrangements, prepares meeting records and tracks follow-up on conclusions and recommendations.
•    Supports partnerships and outreach with United Nations entities, government counterparts, academic institutions and other relevant stakeholders; assists in organizing consultations, expert group meetings and capacity-development activities, and contributes presentations and substantive inputs on assigned topics.
•    Participates in surveys, needs assessments and other data-collection initiatives supporting assigned programmes and country engagement; assists in developing data-collection tools, analyzes responses and prepares findings to inform programme design and implementation.
•    Contributes to the collection, analysis and presentation of data on ESCWA’s country-level engagement and programme results, including inputs to corporate and United Nations system reporting platforms; prepares charts, tables, dashboards and other visual outputs to support planning, reporting and communication of results.
•    Performs other duties as required.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education: A first-level university degree is required; preferably in business administration, management, economics or related field is desirable.

Experience: A minimum of two years of progressively responsible experience in project/programme management, administration or related area is desirable.

Language: English and French are the working languages of the United Nations Secretariat. In addition, Arabic is a working language at ESCWA. For this position, fluency in English is required.
