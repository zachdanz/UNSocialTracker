Org. Setting and Reporting
This post is located within the Central and Southern Africa Division of the Department of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs and Department of Peace Operations (DPPA-DPO) at Headquarters. The Associate Political Affairs Officer reports to the Great Lakes and Southern Africa Team Leader, under the overall guidance of the Director of the Central and Southern Africa Division.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Political Affairs Officer will be responsible for the following duties:

• Researches, collects and analyses information contained in communications and publications received from different sources, including the press, especially the countries in the Southern Africa region as well as Southern Africa Development Community (SADC).

• Drafts, contributes to the preparation of correspondence and various materials relating to the Southern Africa region.

•Maintains up-to-date knowledge of events relating to political issues, in general, and in particular as they affect countries and regional arrangements in the Southern African region.

• Assesses trends which might affect the political situation and the political impact of intra-regional issues.

• Keeps abreast of latest trends and developments in the Southern African region and provides inputs, advice and support on issues related to the political situation to senior officials.

• Monitors national and regional level political developments and provides advice to the DPPA-DPO Liaison Team to SADC and UN agencies active on the ground.

• Provides technical and substantive back up in the implementation of mandates of the Central and Southern Africa Division.

• Liaises with other sectors of the UN, international organizations and governments on substantive, coordination and policy matters.

• Monitors actions taken by intergovernmental groups, UN organizations, government and non-governmental organizations.

• Prepares, monitors and implements projects and programmatic activities, and provides support on human resources issues.

• Performs other related duties as required.

Competencies
PROFESSIONALISM: Knowledge of challenges in the Southern Africa region; ability to identify and analyze political, social and economic problems. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.

TEAMWORK: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others’ ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.

PLANNING & ORGANIZING: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education

A first-level university degree is required, preferably in political science, international relations, international economics, law, public administration or related fields is desirable.

Work Experience

A minimum of two years of progressively responsible experience in political science, international relations, law, disarmament, security, development management, conflict resolution or related areas is desirable.

Experience working in Africa or on African issues is desirable.

Experience in project management is desirable.

Languages

English and French are the two working languages of the United Nations. For this post, fluency in English is required. Knowledge of another UN official language is an advantage.
