Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the United Nations Department of Safety and Security (UNDSS), Specialized Services Branch (SSB), Information Management Unit (IMU). UNDSS is responsible for providing leadership, operational support and oversight to the UN Security Management System (UNSMS) to enable the safest and most efficient conduct of the programmes and activities of the United Nations System. UNDSS’s digital transformation initiative, guided by the UN Data Strategy, aims to modernize security management and accountability tools to support timely data-based critical decision-making, situational awareness and information-sharing and, thus, better enable UN organizations and implementing partners to reach beneficiaries. The overall area of work of Associate Information Management Officer is to assist in the development and implementation of effective data management tools and practices.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority and under the direct supervision of the Programme Management Officer, the incumbent will be responsible for the following duties:

Provides technical support for existing reports, dashboards, or other tools; perform ongoing monitoring and refinement of reports. 
Supports team members with data cleansing from primary or secondary sources and maintains databases/data systems; identifies and analyses errors and inconsistencies in data and provides timely resolutions.
Translate data results into written reports, or presentations to convey information to management; create data visualizations for internal or external use independently.
Develops and maintains applications, automated workflows, dashboards, trackers and AI-enabled tools using Microsoft Power Platform and other approved technologies.
Translates business requirements into user-centered digital solutions and provides related technical support, documentation and user guidance.
Supervises and undertakes archival processing activities by producing descriptive inventories; prepares mark-up of inventories for electronic dissemination; and monitors adherence to description, database and preservation standards.
Supervises General Service staff relating to functions cited above. 
Performs other related duties, as required.
Competencies
Professionalism: Excellent analytical skills with the ability to collect, organize, manage, and disseminate significant amounts of information with attention to detail and accuracy. The ability to analyse, model and interpret data in support of decision-making and convey information succinctly to management. Adept at queries, report writing and presenting findings. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.
Planning& Organizing: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently.
Technological Awareness: Keeps abreast of available technology; understands applicability and limitation of technology to the work of the office; actively seeks to apply technology to appropriate tasks; shows willingness to learn new technology.
Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education: A first level university degree is required, preferably in information science, information systems, computer science or related field.

Experience: Progressively responsible experience in data management, information management, information systems, application development, business process automation, data visualization, ICT or related area is desirable. Experience in designing, developing and maintaining applications, automated workflows, dashboards or other digital solutions using Microsoft Power Platform tools is desirable. Experience in developing and implementing AI-enabled solutions, including conversational assistants or chatbots, is desirable.

Language: English and French are the working languages of the United Nations. For this post fluency in English is required. Knowledge of another UN language is an advantage.
