Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Western Africa Division of the Departments of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs and Peace Operations (DPPA-DPO) at Headquarters. The Associate Political Affairs Officer reports to the Senior Political Affairs Officer/Team Leader of West Africa Team, under the overall guidance of the Director and Deputy Director for the Western Africa Division.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Associate Political Affairs Officer will be responsible for the following duties:

• Monitors political, peace and security events in assigned countries in the Western Africa region; and assesses national and regional trends which might affect the political situation in the countries within his/her purview in the Western Africa Division.
• Prepares, in coordination with the Team Leader and under the overall guidance of the Director and Deputy Director for the Western Africa Division, briefings, talking points, reports, weekly updates and inputs to the Office of the Assistant Secretary-General for Africa, the Office of the Under Secretary-General for Political and Peacebuilding Affairs and the Executive Office of the Secretary-General.
• Drafts correspondence, notes, reports, memoranda, and responses to queries for the review of the Senior Political Affairs Officer.
• Develops and maintains professional relationships, which support and facilitate the work of the Western Africa Division, including with representatives of national governments and international organizations, and other concerned partners, including consultants, business executives, and civil society and academics.
• Arranges and participates in technical, fact-finding, and assessment missions.
• Attends meetings and consultations of the Security Council, the General Assembly, subsidiary organizations, negotiating bodies, conferences, meetings, and/or special missions on issues relevant to the Western Africa region.
• Organizes, supports, or participates in inter-agency meetings and crisis mechanisms relevant to the countries assigned in the Western Africa region.
• Performs other related duties as required.

Competencies
PROFESSIONALISM: Knowledge and understanding of the work of the United Nations, in particular the Departments of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs and Peace Operations. Shows ability to complete in-depth studies and reach conclusions on possible causes and solutions to political problems in specific areas and/or countries. Shows ability to relate to various issues and perspectives to political, social, economic and humanitarian programmes in affected countries and areas. Shows pride in work and in achievements; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work.

TEAMWORK: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others' ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.

PLANNING & ORGANIZING: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently.

Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education

A first-level university degree is required, preferably in political science, international relations, international economics, law, public administration or other related area. 

Work Experience

A minimum of two years of progressively responsible experience in political science, international relations, law, disarmament, security, development management, conflict resolution or related area is desirable.

Experience providing support to a field operation of the United Nations common system (inclusive of peacekeeping, political missions and UN regional commissions, agencies, funds, and programmes) - or a similar international organization, or nongovernmental organization is desirable.

Experience drafting briefing materials and talking points as well as reports and official correspondence is desirable.

Experience working in West Africa or African issues is desirable.

Experience developing and managing projects and budget proposals is desirable.

Language: English and French are the two working languages of the United Nations. For this post, fluency in both English and French is required.
