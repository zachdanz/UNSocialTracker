Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Front Office of the Office of the Under-Secretary-General and Special Adviser on Africa (OSAA). The incumbent reports to the Programme Management Officer (P-4) in the Front Office, who reports directly to the Under-Secretary-General and Special Adviser on Africa.

The Front Office supports the Executive Direction and Management (EDM) functions of OSAA and serves as the central coordination mechanism for the Office. Working closely with the Strategic Management Unit (SMU), the Front Office provides strategic, programmatic, administrative, budgetary and human resources support to ensure effective delivery of OSAA mandates and priorities across its substantive teams. The Front Office also supports cross-cutting initiatives, including the OSAA Policy Innovation Fellowship, and facilitates coordination with Member States, United Nations entities, the African Union, development partners and other stakeholders. OSAA's mandate includes advising the Secretary-General on issues concerning Africa, strengthening coherence and coordination of UN support to Africa, supporting intergovernmental processes and monitoring global commitments to Africa's development.

Responsibilities
Under the supervision of the Programme Management Officer (P-4), the incumbent:

Supports Front Office coordination of OSAA's programme of work and strategic priorities across substantive teams.
Assists in monitoring implementation of senior management decisions and key deliverables, ensuring timely follow-up and reporting.
Supports budget planning, implementation and monitoring activities, including tracking resource utilization and preparation of management reports.
Assists with human resources planning and administration, including recruitment actions, onboarding, staffing reviews, workforce planning and related HR processes.
Supports administrative management functions of the Office in coordination with SMU and relevant Secretariat service providers.
Assists in preparing briefing notes, talking points, background materials, correspondence and presentations for the USG/Special Adviser and senior management.
Supports preparation and coordination of senior-level meetings, missions, events and stakeholder engagements.
Conducts research and analysis related to OSAA priorities, including Agenda 2063, the 2030 Agenda and emerging policy issues affecting Africa.
Supports implementation and coordination of the OSAA Policy Innovation Fellowship and other Front Office-led initiatives.
Liaises with internal and external stakeholders and contributes to knowledge management, reporting and performance monitoring activities.
Performs other duties as required.
