Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is in the Women and Youth Economic Empowerment Section within the Socio-Economic Development Division (SEDD) of the Economic Commission of Africa (ECA). The position is focused on a sub-specialty such as development economics, public economics, gender economics, or technology and development. The Associate Economics Affairs Officer will report to the Chief, Women and Youth Economic Empowerment Section within the Socio-Economic Development Division. 

 

The Associate Economic Affairs Officer is responsible for economic research, policy analysis, intergovernmental processes, and technical cooperation by collecting and analyzing data on gender equality and the economic empowerment of women and youth in Africa, preparing reports, briefing materials, and project documentation, supporting meetings and stakeholder engagements, monitoring development projects, and contributing to data-driven planning, decision-making, and reporting through data analysis and visualization.

The Associate Economic Affairs Officers reports directly to the Chief of Section, Women and Youth Economic Empowerment Section and is under overall supervision of the Director of Socio-Economic Development Division.

Responsibilities
1. Applied Economic Research, modeling and Policy Analysis:

Support the collection, integration, and analysis of data, contribute to identifying trends or patterns, and prepare draft insights through graphs, charts, tables, and reports using data visualization methods to support data‑driven planning, decision‑making, presentation, and reporting.
Contribute to econometric and statistical analysis by supporting the interpretation of outputs and providing inputs to scenario planning, evidence-based decision-making, and programme design.
Contribute to cross-sectoral economic analysis and macroeconomic modeling by gathering data from statistics offices, and International Financial Institutions (IFIs), and other relevant data sources, conducting preliminary analysis and providing inputs to support economic planning.
2. Economic Analysis, Policy formulation and advisory:

Contribute to studies on regional integration, trade corridors, regional value chains, regional investment clusters, and cross-border linkages by analysis and synthesizing findings to support regional development initiatives and cooperation frameworks.
Contribute to the analysis of industrial and innovation clusters by integrating data, literature, and stakeholder inputs to support regional growth through industry cooperation and new technologies, while also ensuring environmental protection.
3. Economic policy analysis and advisory:

Contribute to the development and implementation of models and frameworks that support economic planning and transformation in the context of climate resilience, nature-positive outcomes, pollution reduction, and sustainable natural resource management through conducting data analysis and generating analytical findings.
4. Technical assistance and Capacity building:

Contribute to the design and implemention of technical cooperation activities in partnership with country stakeholders, supporting alignment with local needs and development priorities.
Contribute to policy advisory missions by conducting initial analysis, preparing background materials, and supporting inclusive economic and development planning processes.
5. Intergovernmental support and coordination:

Prepare analytical and assessment inputs on trade agreements by reviewing data, analyzing provisions, and generating initial findings to support trade negotiations.
Contribute to the preparation of substantive inputs to intergovernmental meetings to inform discussions and deliberations on debt and fiscal policy through data analysis and background materials.
Contribute to the preparation of briefing notes, talking points, policy statements, speeches, and presentations by synthesizing inputs and supporting analytical content for high-level engagements and intergovernmental processes.
6. Stakeholder engagement and partnership:

Contribute to the organization of technical meetings and stakeholder workshops by supporting agenda development, stakeholder coordination, and documentation of discussions to facilitate consensus-building and capture lessons learned.
Competencies
Professionalism

Knowledge of economic theories and their application. Ability to identify and develop sources for data collection. Ability to undertake research, analyse data, make recommendations and write reports. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work. Able to analyze and interpret data in support of decision-making and convey resulting information to management.
Communication

Speaks and writes clearly and effectively; listens to others, correctly interprets messages from others and responds appropriately; asks questions to clarify, and exhibits interest in having two-way communication; tailors language, tone, style and format to match audience; demonstrates openness in sharing information and keeping people informed.
Teamwork

Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others’ ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.
Eligibility & Suitability Req
Education:

A first-level university degree is required: preferably in economics or related field is desirable. 
Successful completion of both degree and non-degree programs in data analytics, business analytics or data science programs is desirable.  
Work experience:

A minimum of two years of progressively responsible experience in economic research and analysis, policy formulation, and application of economic principles in development programmes or related areas is desirable.
Skill to communicate complex and technical matters to diverse audiences, orally and in writing, is desirable.
Data analysis skills are desirable.
